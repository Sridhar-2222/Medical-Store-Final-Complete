# BizCore Review: guide for every business type

BizCore is a review platform. The owner (or their staff) enters purchase bills, expenses, revenue, walk-ins,
miscellaneous costs and stock movements, by typing, by photographing bills and printouts, or by importing from
their billing software. BizCore turns that into analysis for any day range, flags what needs checking, and gives
every customer a vault through which the owner stays in touch.

It is not billing software. Billing and stock screens exist only for a client who asks for them (Settings → Screens).

## What every business enters

| Input | How |
|---|---|
| Sales / revenue | Daily numbers screen, photo of the day-end report printout, or import from billing software |
| Collections (cash, UPI, card, other) | Daily numbers, day-end report photo, import |
| Walk-ins (customers, guests, patients) | Daily numbers for morning and evening, day-end report photo, import |
| Purchase bills | Photo (read on the phone, or by AI) with item lines; or typed |
| Expenses (fixed or variable) and miscellaneous | Photo of vouchers, Daily numbers, import |
| Goods received, supplies issued, stock counts, wastage | Photo of challans, issue slips, count sheets; or typed |

## What every owner gets

- Any day to any day: sales, collections, expenses, purchases, profit, walk-ins, average spend per customer
- Day-on-day change in sales, morning vs evening, department by department
- Spot checks on random days or chosen days, compared with the whole period
- Collections short of sales, price rises against earlier bills, short deliveries, wastage, unusual spending
- Purchased vs supplied vs stock in hand, with missing stock in quantity and money
- Stock used against sales, day by day
- Key ratios for the business type with healthy ranges (below)
- Customer vault: records, bills, reminders and WhatsApp messages for every customer; a private link per customer when the shop runs a server

## 🏪 General Business

**Departments:** Sales counter, Operations, Office

**Photograph:** Supplier bills; Expense vouchers; Stock count sheet; Day-end sales report printed by your billing machine

**Enter:** Walk-ins (number of customers) for the morning and the evening; Daily sales and collections per counter; Expenses with fixed or variable; Wastage and miscellaneous

**Owner learns:**
- Profit estimate every day without an accountant
- Collections shortfall by counter and day
- Fixed vs variable cost split to plan pricing
- Any day to any day: sales, expenses, profit, walk-ins, average spend and day-on-day growth
- Stock used against sales, day by day

**Customers get:**
- A personal vault for every customer: bill, document / quote, follow-up, all in one place they can open any time
- Timely WhatsApp messages from you: thank you, follow-up, offer / news, payment reminder
- Bills and payment reminders on WhatsApp
- Consistent pricing because purchase price rises are caught early

**Customer vault keeps:** Bill, Document / quote, Follow-up (reminder: follow up on)

**Ready messages:** Thank you, Follow-up, Offer / news, Payment reminder

| Key number | Healthy range | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Purchases as % of sales | 30–65% | How much of every sale goes back into buying stock or material. |
| Wastage as % of purchases | 0–3% | Money lost to spoiled, damaged or expired goods. |
| Running expenses as % of sales | 0–25% | Rent, salaries, power and other costs against sales. |

## 💊 Pharmacy / Medical Store

**Departments:** Retail counter, Wholesale, Home delivery

**Photograph:** Distributor invoices (batch, expiry, PTR, free qty); Expiry / breakage return slips; Monthly stock count sheet; Day-end sales report printed by your billing machine

**Enter:** Walk-ins (number of customers) for the morning and the evening; Counter, wholesale and delivery sales; Cash / UPI / card collections and patient credit; Expired and damaged stock as wastage; Issues to the delivery boy or branch as supply

**Owner learns:**
- Margin per day and per department (15–25% is healthy)
- Distributor rate rises and cheaper distributors for the same medicine
- Short supply: billed vs received strips
- Expiry loss trend and stock missing from the shelf
- Any day to any day: sales, expenses, profit, walk-ins, average spend and day-on-day growth
- Stock used against sales, day by day

**Customers get:**
- A personal vault for every customer: prescription, bill, allergy / health note, lab report reference, all in one place they can open any time
- Timely WhatsApp messages from you: refill reminder, order ready, thank you, offer / news, payment reminder
- Bill on WhatsApp for every patient
- Refill and payment reminders on WhatsApp
- Fresher stock with fewer near-expiry medicines
- Faster service because popular medicines are not out of stock

**Customer vault keeps:** Prescription (reminder: refill due), Bill, Allergy / health note, Lab report reference

**Ready messages:** Refill reminder, Order ready, Thank you, Offer / news, Payment reminder

| Key number | Healthy range | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Purchases as % of sales | 70–85% | Below 70% may mean missing purchase bills; above 85% means thin margins (healthy margin is 15–25%). |
| Expired / damaged as % of purchases | 0–1.5% | Above 1.5% means slow movers are being over-bought or expiry returns are not claimed. |
| Collected as % of sales | 95–100% | Below 95% means too much credit to patients or unrecorded cash. |
| Delivery costs as % of delivery sales | 0–6% | Home delivery should cost under 6% of what it sells. |
