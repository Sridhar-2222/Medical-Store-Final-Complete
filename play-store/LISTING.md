**Full description** (max 4000):
```
Fingertrip.biz AI shows a business owner where the business stands every day: sales, spending and profit, without an accountant and without changing your billing.

Built for restaurants, hotels, pharmacies, grocery stores, salons, clinics, clothing and electronics shops, hardware and building-material traders, bakeries, garages, gyms and service businesses.

MAIN HEADERS
• Accounts: sales by department, purchases, salaries, fixed costs, maintenance, variable and sudden expenses, cash in hand, profit & loss
• Customers: a vault for every customer, discounts, special offers, reminders and WhatsApp messages
• Store: every purchase bill by category
• Reports: profit & loss, alerts, sales and spending reports, spot checks
• Staff: staff list, documents and monthly salaries
• Business setup: departments, store categories, fixed costs, videos, backup

PHOTO OF EVERY BILL
Take a photo of a purchase bill and Fingertrip.biz AI reads the supplier, bill number, date and total. Photograph your billing machine's day-end report and it fills sales, cash, UPI and card. Photos are read on the phone, even without internet.

PROFIT EVERY DAY
Sales − purchases = gross profit. Then variable, maintenance and sudden expenses, and a day's share of fixed costs like rent, electricity and salaries, give your net profit for any day, week or month.

ALERTS THAT MATTER
Cash short at closing, money collected less than sales, spending above sales, sudden expenses, purchases above the 4-week average, fixed costs going up.

CUSTOMER VAULT
Every customer gets a vault with your business name on it: prescriptions, warranties, service dates, orders and documents, reminders and messages. Send refill, service, renewal and birthday reminders and special offers on WhatsApp in one tap.

WORKS ON YOUR PHONE
Everything stays on your phone and works offline. Optionally connect to your own office computer to share numbers between phones. Optional AI reading (OpenAI or Google Gemini, with your own key) for handwritten bills.

No ads. No account needed.
```
